## Purpose

Make the persistent basketball universe easy to advance and compelling to explore through the stories, records, champions, dynasties, and career paths it creates.

## Direction

Season Atlas: a premium global sports annual where the current simulation moment is always visible, while history is organized as connected routes across people, teams, competitions, and seasons. A dark game rail anchors familiar navigation; warm atlas surfaces hold the archive. The signature move is a clear directional time grammar: current action at the top, historical champions and record trails extending below.
