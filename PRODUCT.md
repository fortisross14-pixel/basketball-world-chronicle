# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Basketball simulation fans who want to observe a long-running alternate basketball universe. Their primary job is to advance time, then trace the human and competitive stories that emerge across the world.

## Product Purpose

Basketball World Chronicle simulates a persistent global basketball sandbox. Players advance the universe and discover its stories: record chases, top scorers, new dynasties, surprising champions, and careers that cross college, the EuroLeague, and the NBA.

## Positioning

Rather than asking players to manage one club, Chronicle makes an interconnected basketball world itself the protagonist: each simulation creates a browsable history across countries, competitions, teams, and careers.

## Operating Context

Players periodically simulate calendar time, inspect the next fixture or season state, and follow linked people, teams, competitions, records, and historical events. They return to see how old storylines evolved and uncover new ones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing persistent-universe simulation, save slots, world data, routes, player/team/competition history, live-game presentation, and connected navigation must remain functional. The app is a Vite + React web application.

## Brand Commitments

The experience should feel like a premium basketball universe game and make story discovery, not administrative data entry, the center of the experience.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working game engine, real-world team badges, flags, player portraits, competition data, and linked game records. Product claims should remain grounded in the simulated data; no fictional external proof is needed.

## Product Principles

- Make the next story obvious after every simulation step.
- Let players travel naturally from a headline to the people, teams, and competitions behind it.
- Treat history and records as living drama, not archive clutter.
- Preserve the depth of the world while giving casual checking sessions a clear path.
- Reward curiosity with coherent visual hierarchy and rich basketball identity.
